# Business Model: Independent Game Software Production Studio

## Classification

- Repository: `cloud-itonami-jsic-3914`
- JSIC: `3914` ゲームソフトウェア業 (Game Software Production)
- Industry: independent game software production studio
- Social impact: creative-livelihoods, cultural-expression, local-jobs

## Customer

- publishers and platform holders commissioning a title or a port
- IP owners commissioning a game adaptation of an existing property
- studios subcontracting a vertical slice, a prototype, or live-ops content
- brands and campaigns commissioning a promotional or in-world title

## Offer

- design-as-data: the gameSpec is the deliverable, and it is reviewable before
  a line of the build exists
- build production from a spec against a shared runtime
- balance and consistency review (`kotoba-lang/game-production`) as a
  standalone service, including on a client's own spec
- generated audio production — music bed and the cue set a build needs
- release operations: rating submission, asset-licence clearance, store
  delivery

## Revenue

- per-title production fee
- monthly live-ops / content retainer
- design review engagement (spec audit, no build)
- revenue share on published titles

## Trust Controls

- no publication without an age rating (CERO/IARC) **and** asset-licence
  clearance covering every third-party or generated asset in the build
- no build from a spec with unresolved consistency findings without human
  sign-off — a dangling reference does not fail, it ships broken
- no build of a design with no endgame without human sign-off — the studio
  will not deliver a game whose every run ends by attrition
- no release of a build whose generation legs fell back to placeholder or
  silence
- title, rating, licence and build records are auditable, not editable

## What this business does not sell

It does not sell an opinion about whether a game is fun. The governor's design
gate is arithmetic — consistency and sustainable dps against spawn pressure —
because those are the properties that can be decided without a player. Anything
softer stays a human judgement and is priced as design review, not automated.
